# eBay title rules

- Keep titles at or below the configured character limit.
- Put `for` before vehicle makes.
- Lead with the product's core keyword.
- Fetch eBay search suggestions for the core keyword and preserve their returned order as a search-habit signal, not verified search volume.
- Add only operator-approved suggestions. Merge novel words in ranked order and omit lower-priority words when the 80-character budget is exhausted.
- Never infer product features from a suggestion; attributes such as sensors, heating, or cruise control require product verification before selection.
- Prefer make, model or family members, then fitment years.
- Do not repeat the same keyword.
- Discovery mixed titles include as many market-ranked remaining models as fit without exceeding the limit.
- Every SKU with at least two distinct compatible models requires one full-fitment Mixed title ordered by descending market rank.
- Do not pull an existing Core model into a Discovery mixed title unless the account strategy explicitly allows overlapping Compatibility pools.
